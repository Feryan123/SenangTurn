How SenangTurn works

A senior caregiver turns a bedbound partner alone, with no electricity and no drilling. Step through it below, or drag the crank yourself. The view is from the foot of the bed.

Crank gear
  1. Start flatLower fully and slacken the rope. Never slide the pulley while someone is lifted.
  2. Slide and lockPull the waist-height cord. The pulley rolls above the edge to lift and the spring pin clicks in.
  3. ClipClip the rope to the bar on that edge of the sheet.
  4. CrankTurn the handle. The edge lifts and the person rolls slowly to the other side. Stop at the 30° mark.
  5. LowerRelease the ratchet gently until flat, then slide the sheet out.

Reach the Crank step to turn the handle yourself.
